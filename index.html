About LuminaDia
Explainable AI framework for iris-based non-invasive diabetes detection
Framework Architecture

LuminaDia integrates four state-of-the-art deep learning architectures into a unified, transparent clinical diagnostic pipeline designed for detecting diabetic retinopathy from iris images without any invasive procedures.

SwinUNetV2 · Segmentation
Transformer-based iris ROI segmentation, isolating medically significant regions with 98.6% accuracy. Filters eyelids, reflections, and irrelevant tissue.
Vision Transformer (ViT) · Global features
Processes 1,296 patches (7×7) per image. Self-attention captures long-range dependencies and global iris texture patterns indicative of diabetic changes.
EfficientNetV2-L · Local texture
Hierarchical CNN for fine-grained microstructural feature extraction. Captures crypt distortions, fiber irregularities, and pigment variations.
ConvNeXtV2 · Classification
Classifies diabetic severity grades 0–4. Combines local and global representations for accurate multi-class differentiation across DR stages.
Grad-CAM · Explainability
Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping visualizes which iris regions drive each prediction. Bridges AI decision-making with clinical reasoning, providing transparent heatmaps that clinicians can verify against anatomical knowledge.
